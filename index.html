About me

I'm a Senior Analyst focused on business intelligence and data visualization. At Nagarro I work on US healthcare analytics, building Tableau and Power BI dashboards that track brand performance, sales, market share, campaign promotion and patient engagement for business stakeholders.

My work covers the full path from raw data to decision: moving data from AWS S3 into Databricks and PostgreSQL with Matillion, preparing it with Tableau Prep, analyzing it with SQL, validating it with quality checks and governing it in Collibra, then presenting it in dashboards that stay fast on high data volumes. I work in Agile sprints and stay close to stakeholders so every report answers a real business question.
